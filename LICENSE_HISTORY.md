# License history and transition

Repository: https://github.com/atoosabiglari-PM/atoosabiglari-PM

Baseline before this change: [`be0b1e12a143bc505a19258229ef4eb30291a13b`](https://github.com/atoosabiglari-PM/atoosabiglari-PM/tree/be0b1e12a143bc505a19258229ef4eb30291a13b).
Previous top-level terms: **no top-level license**.
Current owner-controlled original materials: **CC BY-NC 4.0**, with the
additional permissions in [JUDGING_PERMISSION.md](JUDGING_PERMISSION.md).

The policy takes effect at the Git commit that first introduces these files.
This is a forward license change, not a rewrite of Git history or a claim that
earlier releases were always restricted. Existing tags, historical commits,
copies, and previously granted permissions are not withdrawn or invalidated.

No top-level LICENSE was present at the baseline. That observation does not
mean that third-party files, dependencies, platform rights, or separate
permissions lacked their own terms.

Any permissions already granted under prior terms or separate agreements remain
unaffected. The new noncommercial sharing permissions apply only within the
owner's rights and the scope stated in LICENSING.md.

Historical license texts are retained as evidence of prior terms and for
compliance where they still apply. They do not grant an alternative permissive
license for new contributions first released under the current terms.
